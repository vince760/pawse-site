Frequently asked questions

How does Pawse detect barking?
It analyzes microphone audio on your device in real time during a session. Audio is never recorded or uploaded — it’s processed in memory and discarded.
What does the cue do?
When a bark is detected, Pawse can play a short, low-volume sound to gently interrupt barking, within the cooldown and max-cue limits you set. It’s an interruption aid, not punishment — pair it with calm rewards.
I selected the high-frequency cue but I can’t hear it.
That’s expected. The high-frequency cue is near-ultrasonic (up to ~20 kHz) and most adults can’t hear it. Judge it by your dog’s reaction. Output also depends on your phone’s speaker, which gets weaker at high frequencies.
Is my data private?
Yes. There are no servers, accounts, ads, or trackers. Optional session summaries are stored only on your device. See the Privacy Policy.
Does it work in the background or with the screen off?
Pawse listens during an active, foreground session. Keep the app open with the screen on for best results.
How do I delete my data?
Turn off Session Logging in Settings, or delete the app to remove all locally stored session data.
