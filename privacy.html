Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 4, 2026

Pawse (“the app”) is a humane crate-training aid for dogs. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your data. Your privacy is central to how the app is designed.

Summary

  • The app processes microphone audio on your device, in real time, only during an active training session, to detect likely barking.
  • Raw audio is never recorded, saved, transmitted, or uploaded anywhere. Audio is analyzed in memory and discarded.
  • The app has no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers.
  • The app does not collect personal information and does not require an account.

Microphone

When you start a session, the app listens through the microphone to estimate whether your dog is barking, so it can optionally play a short, low-volume training cue. This processing happens entirely on your device. The audio stream is not stored and never leaves the device.

Data stored on your device

If you enable Session Logging in Settings, the app saves session summaries (start and end times, counts of detected barks and played cues, quiet-streak durations, and any notes you type) in local storage on your device. This data:

  • stays on your device,
  • is not transmitted to us or anyone else,
  • is removed when you delete the app.

You can turn Session Logging off at any time in Settings.

Children

The app is a tool for pet owners, is not directed at children, and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new “Last updated” date.
